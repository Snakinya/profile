Zhiwei Lin

PhD Student, Computer Science, National University of Singapore

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the National University of Singapore, advised by Prof. Chengpeng Wang. My research focuses on agent security, with an emphasis on discovering, evaluating, and defending against real-world AI risks. My broader research interests include systems security, network security, vulnerability analysis, and program analysis. I am also a core member of the CTF team 0x401.

I am always happy to discuss research ideas and warmly welcome collaborations, so please feel free to reach out.
